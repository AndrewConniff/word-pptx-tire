# Analyst to Outlook to Teams Workflow

Classification: Public

1. Open `Canada-Wide-Winter-Tire-Sales-Weather-2023-2026.xlsx` in Excel.
2. Ask Analyst/Copilot for a chart comparing weekly tire sales vs snowfall by region.
3. Copy chart into Outlook and ask Copilot to draft a concise executive summary.
4. Paste same summary/chart into Teams and ask Copilot to rewrite for store teams.
5. Keep owner/date actions explicit in both communications.
